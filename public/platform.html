For the curious

The AI isn't in the OS. The AI is the OS.

You never have to think about any of this — that's the whole point. But if you like to know what's under the calm, here's how MikeOS actually works.

The idea

One intelligence, living your day with you.

For forty years, a phone was a drawer of apps you had to open, arrange and manage. MikeOS turns that inside out. Instead of apps you operate, MikeOS runs a living intelligence that understands your day — your places, your people, your rhythms — and quietly takes care of the rest. The interface isn't a grid of icons. It's a conversation, and a thousand small things handled before you ask.

Underneath, every capability is its own autonomous agent: a small, focused mind for your photos, your travel, your home, your music. Each one senses its corner of your world, thinks, acts, remembers, and talks to the others — so a single moment, like arriving somewhere new, ripples through everything at once.

How it works

Each agent runs the same gentle loop, continuously — not waiting for you to tap.

On your device first

Phone and computer as one

Your devices share a single, continuous mind. Start on one, finish on the other, without a seam or a sync button.

Agents that cooperate

A photo you take, a place you arrive, a song you love — one event, understood by every agent that cares about it.

Yours to carry forward

The promises

No ads. Ever. In any MikeOS app.

Your attention isn't a product we sell. MikeOS apps will never show you an ad. Third-party apps you choose to install may — but any app carrying the MikeOS Certified badge is guaranteed ad-free, respectful of your data, and honest about what it does.

Privacy by design

What happens on your device stays on your device wherever it can. Nothing is quietly mined to build a profile of you.

You own it, fully

One identity, held by you. Your data is scoped to you and portable — the account answers to you and no one else.

Build on it

Developers can build agents that plug into the same shared intelligence — and by earning MikeOS Certified, promise their users the same thing we do: no ads, real privacy, no dark patterns.
