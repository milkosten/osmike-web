Agents that act for you

Say what you want done. Your agents do it.

In Mesh you get your own AI agents. Talk to them in Mesh or on Telegram. They work across your calendar, mail, files, tasks, CRM and the other services, and they answer you when the job is done.

Ask for help with a wish and Mesh puts the right agents on it, or pick a ready team of five from Rolez.
