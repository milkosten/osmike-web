OsMike Connect builds integrations on request, from public data to your own business tools.

Give the key in the chat

Paste the service's API key in the chat with your agent, or sign in to the service. The key is hidden in the chat and stored in your own vault.

⚙️

Built and tested for you

OsMike builds the integration and tests it with your key, often within minutes. Then your agents can use it.

🛡️

You stay in control

Your agents never see the key: every call is made for them on our servers. You decide whether they may only read or also change things.
